#2 Work for it

I love short cuts. If I can put in less time and energy to complete a task, I’m all about it. Nowadays, we are surrounded by these quick and easy tools. I can take a picture of my food and in seconds, boom done – ive just shared it with the world.

But I’m thinking there are just certain things that requires you to take the long and tedious road. Because maybe we think we want to achieve a goal but the matter of fact is we need to go through the process of getting there. And it actually requires a bit of effort – shocker.

It’s come to my realization that even exploring what to do next in itself requires a significant amount of work. Especially if you have all the freedom and no institution or other external factors motivating you to hit a deadline. It’s just me and myself, and the endless possibilities. And it’s rather unsettling.

With freedom, you have to be even more motivated and diligent to get things done. Whether it is working out, finding your hobbies, connecting with friends. No longer is someone telling you what to do and making you accountable with deadlines.

The world is nothing but chaos, and we find meaning from it through our efforts.
