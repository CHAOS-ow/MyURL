Sometimes it's right in front of you, that perfect job. You're actually doing it right now, but being in the eye of the storm, you forget the impact you make in the world. It's interesting to see how our jobs have evolved after the industrial revolution and how our careers have become a separate entity from who we are. We cannot be boxed into a single role. We are more than our job description, filled with creativity and ideas. If your job does not ask for what you are made of, show them what you can achieve. Bring meaning to your job.
