Is it that the truest form of art is boundless? or is it bound to an idea colored within its lines to perfection? Boundaries exists to bend them, it's my nature to break them. Like it is to redefine who I am. Who am I? I am the benevolent, I am the courageous, I am of expression. I've become a wandering target. chaos adds to more and ends up being nothing but matter dispersing through a dusky air. Just floating away in life. Until maybe some force grabs it, attracts it. But what life? who's life? and we go round and round...
