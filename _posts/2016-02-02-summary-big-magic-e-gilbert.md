**My notes on Big Magic by Elizabeth Gilbert:**

- Ideas are a living force
  - Ideas are driven to be made manifest either by you or someone else
  - Sometimes it's not the right time when you have an idea - it's ok to move on.
  - Someone else might have the same idea but were the ones to follow through with it - and that's ok.
- Follow your curiosity and remain open. Have a sense of wonder.
  - Acknowledge your fears but don't let it lead your creative life.
  - Let your ego serve you instead of it controlling you.
- Your art does not have to be original. It does not have to be important, and it does not have to be perfect.
  - "Done is better than good." - Big Magic
  - It's never too late.
  - "If you argue for your limitations you get to keep them. But if you argue for your possibilities,you get to create them." - Kelly Lee Phipps
  - All ideas have already been done before, but it has not been done by you.
  - Approach with a stubborn gladness
  - "What will make you finish is not self-discipline, but self-forgiveness" - Liz Gilbert
- Is it worth it in the end?
  - Every endeavor has its downfalls. It's a matter of what you do is worth dealing with the negative aspects that come with it.
  - When you're stuck try Combinatory play - the act of opening up one mental channel through something else.
- Live in your own creative world away from what other people think
  - "It ain't what they call you; it's what you answer to." - WC Fields

**My thoughts:**

Absolutely adored the book and is one that I would keep in my bookshelf to re-read. E Gilbert has a refreshing way of inspiring others to follow their passion. Through her witty writing, her book focuses on the creative process and its struggles which have not been clearly addressed before.
