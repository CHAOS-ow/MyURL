**My collection of memorable quotes from The Walk (2015)**

- Why do you walk on the wire? Why do you tempt fate? Why do you chase...DEATH? But I do not think of it this way. LIFE! For me, walking on the wire...is life. C'est la vie.
- No matter where I was going, or what I was doing... I was always searching for the perfect place to hang my rope.
- It looks so... Beautiful, yes... And dangerous. And completely insane... And you are a madman. [Yes, I am mad. But it's my dream.] Then if it is your dream, you must do it.
- Most wire walkers, they die when they arrive. They think they have arrived... But they are still on the wire. If  you have three steps to do, and if you do those steps arrogantly... if you think you are invincible... you are going to die! Tu vas mourir.
- Now I know what the void is... I'm a wire-walker. The void is my domain. But not this void. But somehow I gather the strength to whisper... whisper so the demons won't hear...It's impossible... But i'll do it.
- You cannot lie on stage. The audience will always feel what is inside your heart.
- Annie, my head is full of... doubts. When it comes time for me to step on the wire... to confront the void. I'm not sure I'll be able to make the first step. [You're heart will tell you what to do. Your accomplices...and your towers will be there to support you.]
- The dressing room is where the transformation takes place...where the disguised impostor...the intruder... Becomes the performer. The artist.
- Before I took that first step, I felt that Jeff no longer existed. My tower was deserted. I felt that the horizon was no longer pat of what we call "the rotundity of the earth." The wire was a floating line to infinity. I no longer heard the sounds of New York. Everything fell silent. I was about to step into an unknown world... "The Void."
- The moment my entire weight is on that wire, I feel immediately a feeling that I know by heart... I feel the wire... I feel the support of the Towers.
- I was walking across a beautiful sea of unknown dimension. Gravity did not exist. I was among the clouds.
- And I arrive at the North Tower feeling the most immense elation... The most intense joy that I ever felt in my life.
- I'm going to remain there an eternity, drinking that void...and being called by the Towers to dance on that wire. And I remember being overcome by the most profound feeling of awareness, of consciousness...of gratitude.
- I salute the Towers. And the wire, and my accomplices...and of course, I salute New York. And I'm so taken with my emotions... I had completely forgotten, this is actually a performance.
- I look down... And it was... It was beautiful. I was calm and beautiful, and serene, and "not-dangerous".
- I cannot end this walk with a feeling of dishonor...Feeling as if I've been assailed by the gods. No, I will only leave my magnificent wire, and my beautiful towers... With my head held high.
- [Why did you do this?] There is no "why." Just because... When I see a beautiful place to put my wire, I cannot resist.

**My thoughts**:

Truly inspirational story that leaves you with the courage to live your dreams. The movie brings Philip's dreams to the mainstream audience, allowing us to share in his journey. The original documentary warrants a watch for those looking for more.
