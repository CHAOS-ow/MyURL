I met with one of my professors/mentors who is a famous guy in our field. He's not one of those admire-from-a-distance kind of person, he's personable and is truly there to mentor anyone and inspire people to fulfill their potential. What a free-spirited force of nature he is.

What my mentor really showed me was not the path to take or what to do, but that there's not much in the way of doing what you want to do other than yourself. His adventures and life stories had a 'Just do it' attitude that made life a crazy and open journey - opposed to the straight assigned path that I have been on for the past few years. When you start realizing that you can color outside the lines and create your own art, it's exhilarating.

That excitement is only that way because it is also fearful. What if I fail? What about my obligations? What if I waste time? I've been at this stage for a while and have realized that these mental barriers have become a reality in my life. But when you give yourself no other options, you make it happen. No more what if's, Just Do It.
