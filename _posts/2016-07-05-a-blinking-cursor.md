One of the more anxiety-provoking moments is a blinking cursor. It doesn't quite tell you what to be afraid of outright, but like a Japanese horror film, it has a subtle way of nagging your insides. Nagging at the fact that you have a blank screen, provoking anxiety knowing the possibilities are limitless. 'Cuz the freedom comes without rules, and highly depends on your own creative mind, your own set of rules that you've established. And dare you express your voice.

It fights against what you're expected to do, or what you think you're expected to do. The outside pressures unconsciously or consciously soften your own voice to creativity, to originality. And what a hard task it is to follow that small voice.

When you find it and follow it, however, it's powerful. It's the power core to Iron man - it is the essence of what makes him super, and you fabulous. You no longer want to stray from your own voice because it felt just right, at ease, and a quiet confidence brews. It becomes easier and easier to find that voice and the follow it, and you just become yourself. And the world needs you.

So create, go wild with that blinking cursor. Dare to express yourself.
