*#1. More Laughter in my life*

At some point we all lose our child-like innocence. Life becomes stale and redundant and no longer playing house in a cardboard box feels like that imaginary kingdom. Remember that excitement buying your first nintendo system? (thanks dad, I will always remember you). I spent hours venturing into the new world that I unboxed.

Somewhere in my life I lost that excitement. Maybe I was a serious child from the start. I was always too pensive for my age. Even now I feel like an old-soul. And I guess, although I hate to admit, I am stoic and serious at work (but easy-going). We’re all a blend of characteristics. Like finely aged-wine, I’m have an easy-going base with overtones of seriousness and moments of excitement and laughter. (What is your wine personality?)

Maybe it’s the work I do or just working in general. My job requires that I am serious and responsible. It augments the serious overtones in my personality, and like aged-wine it has solidified them well. But hey I need some laughter in my life. After all, it is the year of the monkey – what better way to spend 2016 with laughter?
